Ranking power (ROC curve)

Higher and further left is better. The diagonal is a coin flip. AUC = area under this curve; banks report Gini = 2·AUC − 1.

Are the probabilities honest? (calibration)

Each dot is a tenth of applicants. On the diagonal = "said 10%, and 10% defaulted". Pricing and the cut-off formula both need this.

Segments

No label, just groups: who are our customers, and where is the opportunity?

K-means on five features of the held-out applicants. On Safaricom data the same method runs on ward or H3-cell aggregates (k ≥ 15 per cell), never on individuals.

The groups, squashed onto two axes (PCA)

How many groups? (elbow and silhouette)

Inertia keeps falling as K grows. Pick the K where it stops falling fast and the silhouette (how well-separated the groups are) holds up.

The KES 380M jersey question needs causal measurement, not clustering

Difference-in-differences: compare new-user growth in wards with high exposure (Nairobi United fan base) against similar wards without, before and after the shirt deal. Change the example numbers.

Verdict

Not linear regression vs XGBoost. Three tiers, one profit test.

How a decline is explained

  • Top 3 reason codes from the model, in plain words.
  • A counterfactual: "repay on time for two months and you qualify".
  • Grey zone near the cut-off: starter loan or human review, so a model error never costs a good customer outright.
  • Meets the Data Protection Act's rules on decisions made only by automated processing.

What we need to decide together

  • Target: default = 30 or 60 days past due?
